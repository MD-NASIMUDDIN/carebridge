Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect your personal information.

Draft policy: please have this reviewed before it is relied on.

CareBridge Support Services (“we”) handles personal information in line with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles.

What we collect

Your name and contact details, information about the support you are looking for, and any NDIS plan details you choose to share with us.

Why we collect it

To respond to your enquiry, to provide and coordinate supports, and to meet our legal and NDIS obligations.

Who we share it with

Only with your consent, or where the law requires or allows it, for example to the NDIA or the NDIS Quality and Safeguards Commission.

Website enquiries

When you use the enquiry form, it opens your email app with your message ready to send. Your enquiry is then held in our email system.

Keeping it safe

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from misuse, loss and unauthorised access.

Access and correction

You can ask to see or correct the information we hold about you at any time.

Questions

Contact us on 0461 468 914 or armstrongtanyannah@gmail.com.
