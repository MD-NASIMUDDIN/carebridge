Feedback & Complaints

We welcome compliments, suggestions and complaints. They help us do better.

How to tell us

Call 0461 468 914 or email armstrongtanyannah@gmail.com. You can bring a family member, friend or advocate to support you.

What happens next

  • We acknowledge your feedback.
  • We look into it fairly and respectfully.
  • We tell you the outcome and what we will change.

Not happy with the outcome?

You can contact the NDIS Quality and Safeguards Commission on 1800 035 544 or at ndiscommission.gov.au. You do not need to raise it with us first.
