About CareBridge

Locally based in Dalby, supporting NDIS participants and families across the Western Downs.

Bridging support. Building independence. Creating better lives.

CareBridge Support Services is a Dalby-based NDIS provider. We work alongside participants and their families to put the right supports in place, so everyday life feels more manageable and more your own.

We keep things plain and personal: no jargon, no pressure, just a conversation about what matters to you.

What guides us

Listen first

Your goals, routines and preferences come before any plan we suggest.

Choice and control

Decisions about your supports stay with you. We explain the options clearly.

Local and personal

We live and work in the Western Downs, and we know the community we support.

Ready for a plan that actually fits your life?
