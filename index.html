NDIS Guide

How your plan actually gets managed

NDIS funding can be managed three different ways, and honestly, most people we sit down with aren't sure which applies to them. Here's the plain-English version.

SELF-MANAGED

You're in full control

You pay providers yourself and claim the cost back from the NDIA. It's more paperwork, but it opens the door to any provider, registered or not.

PLAN-MANAGED

Someone else handles the paperwork

An independent plan manager pays your invoices and tracks your budget, so you keep freedom of choice without the admin headache.

NDIA-MANAGED

The NDIA handles it directly

The NDIA pays registered providers like us directly on your behalf. It's the simplest option, but it does mean sticking with registered providers such as CareBridge.

ⓘ

Genuinely not sure which one is yours? You're in good company, as most participants we meet aren't either. Our support coordination team will go through your plan with you and help you choose (or switch to) whichever option actually makes your life easier.
